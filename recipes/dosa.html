Dosa

Description

Dosa is a popular South Indian dish made from a fermented batter of rice and lentils. It is typically served with chutney or sambar.

  • 3 cups Dosa rice or parboiled rice
  • 1 cup Whole white urad dal (black gram without skin)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Fenugreek seeds (methi)
  • 2 tablespoons Poha (flattened rice, optional for crispiness)
  • Water (for soaking and grinding)
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil or ghee for cooking the dosas

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice and urad dal separately. Soak the rice in water, and soak the urad dal along with fenugreek seeds in a separate bowl for 4 to 5 hours.
  2. Drain the urad dal and grind it with cold water into a smooth, fluffy paste. Transfer to a large mixing bowl.
  3. Grind the rice with soaked poha into a slightly coarse batter, adding water in small increments.
  4. Combine both batters together with your hands, add salt, and mix thoroughly.
  5. Cover the bowl and keep it in a warm place to ferment for 8 to 12 hours until the batter rises and turns bubbly.
  6. Heat a flat non-stick or cast-iron tawa on medium heat. Sprinkle a few drops of water to test the heat and wipe it clean with a kitchen towel.
  7. Pour a ladleful of batter into the center and quickly spread it in a circular spiral motion outwards to make a thin crepe.
  8. Drizzle a teaspoon of oil or ghee around the edges and cook on medium flame until the base turns golden and crisp.
  9. Fold the dosa and remove it from the tawa. Serve immediately with hot sambar and coconut chutney.
