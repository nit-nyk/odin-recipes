Lasanga

Home

lasanga is a delicious italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the meat and sauce.
  2. Boil the noodles.
  3. Layer the noodles, sauce, and cheese.
  4. Bake in the oven for 30 minutes.
