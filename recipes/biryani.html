Description

Chicken Biryani is a classic savory rice dish made with spiced marinated chicken, fragrant basmati rice, caramelized onions, and warm spices.

It is traditionally slow-cooked using the "dum" method, which traps the steam inside the pot and infuses every grain of rice with deep aroma and flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes)
  • 500g Chicken pieces
  • 1 cup Plain yogurt (curd)
  • 2 large Onions, thinly sliced and fried golden brown
  • 2 tablespoons Ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon Red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon Turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon Garam masala powder
  • Whole spices (bay leaf, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon stick)
  • A few strands of saffron soaked in warm milk
  • Fresh mint and coriander leaves, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons Ghee or cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, salt, and half of the fried onions for at least 1 hour.
  2. Boil a large pot of salted water with whole spices. Add soaked basmati rice and cook until it is 70% done, then drain the water completely.
  3. In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat ghee and cook the marinated chicken over medium heat until it is partially cooked and the oil starts to separate.
  4. Layer the partially cooked rice evenly over the chicken layer in the pot.
  5. Top the rice with saffron milk, remaining fried onions, chopped mint leaves, and coriander leaves.
  6. Seal the pot with a tight-fitting lid or foil and cook on very low flame (Dum) for 20 to 25 minutes.
  7. Let it rest for 10 minutes, gently fluff the rice with a fork, and serve hot with raita.
